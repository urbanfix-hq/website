How it works

From a photo to a fixed street

  1. Report

    A photo and the phone's GPS. The address is found automatically.

  2. Route

    The right authority and provider, from the place and the type of problem.

  3. Fix

    Providers plan the work; field teams post progress and proof on site.

  4. Close the loop

    The authority closes the report and the citizen sees it fixed.
